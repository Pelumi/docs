Nomba Demo Bank

A sandbox banking app that mirrors the Nomba app's transfer flow. Use it to play a customer paying into one of your SIMULATED sandbox virtual accounts.

  1. Create a simulated virtual account in the sandbox and copy its number (it starts with 0)
  2. Tap Transfer on the home screen
  3. Type the account number. The name is looked up on Nombank MFB
  4. Enter an amount and confirm
  5. Enter any 4-digit PIN

Your webhook URL gets payment_success, the same as a real transfer. Use the transaction ref on the receipt to requery it.

No real money moves.
