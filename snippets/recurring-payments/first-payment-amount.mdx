## Choose the amount for the first payment

If the first payment is the customer's first bill, such as the first month of a subscription, charge the real amount.

If you only need the payment to save the card, charge a small amount. Then [refund it](/docs/products/accept-payment/refund-checkout-order), or give the customer the same value in your app, such as wallet credit. Nomba doesn't set a minimum amount, but some banks decline very small card payments, so test the amount you plan to use.

<Tip>
  Set `tokenizeCard` only when you plan to charge the card again. Tell the customer you'll save their card and how you'll use it, and get their agreement before they pay.
</Tip>
