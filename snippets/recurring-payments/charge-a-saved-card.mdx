**Step 3 of 5.** When a payment is due, send the card token and the order details to Nomba. The customer doesn't need to be on your site, unless their bank asks for an OTP.

## Send the charge

```bash
curl --request POST \
  --url https://api.nomba.com/v1/checkout/tokenized-card-payment \
  --header 'Authorization: Bearer <access_token>' \
  --header 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  --header 'accountId: <accountId>' \
  --data '{
    "tokenKey": "7628788443",
    "order": {
      "orderReference": "sub-2026-11-0001",
      "customerId": "cus_48213",
      "customerEmail": "ada@example.com",
      "amount": "5000.00",
      "currency": "NGN"
    }
  }'
```

| Field | Required | Description |
|---|---|---|
| `tokenKey` | Yes | The card token you stored in step 2. |
| `order.amount` | Yes | The amount to charge, as a string with two decimal places, for example `"5000.00"`. |
| `order.currency` | Yes | The currency code, for example `NGN`. |
| `order.customerEmail` | Yes | The email the card was saved with. A different email is rejected. |
| `order.orderReference` | Recommended | Your unique reference for **this** charge, up to 50 characters. Use a new one every time, and keep it. You need it to submit an OTP and to verify the charge. Nomba generates one if you leave it out, but then you can't safely retry. |
| `order.customerId` | No | Your ID for the customer. |

Nomba credits the charge to your main account.

<Note>
  Sub-account (`order.accountId`) and split (`order.splitRequest`) settings aren't applied to charges on a saved card yet. `callbackUrl` isn't needed either, because there's no page to redirect the customer to.
</Note>

## Read the response

Check `code`, `data.status` and `data.otpRequired` to see what happened:

| `code` | `data.status` | `data.otpRequired` | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `"00"` | `true` | `false` | The bank approved the charge. | <a href={`${props.base}/confirm-the-charge`}>Confirm the charge</a> before you give value. |
| `"00"` | `false` | `true` | The bank wants the customer to approve the charge with an OTP. The charge is pending. | Save `data.transactionId` and <a href={`${props.base}/complete-otp`}>complete the OTP challenge</a>. |
| `"00"` | `false` | `false` | The bank declined the charge. `data.message` says why. | See [When a charge fails](#when-a-charge-fails). |
| `"400"` | `false` | `false` | Nomba rejected the request before charging the card. `data.message` says why. | Fix the request. See [When a charge fails](#when-a-charge-fails). |

<Warning>
  Don't use `description` to decide what happened. It says `"tokenized transaction completed successfully"` whenever Nomba reached the bank, even when the charge was declined or is still waiting for an OTP.
</Warning>

An OTP is the only challenge you can complete on a saved-card charge. If the bank asks for a 3D Secure check instead, the charge fails, and the response has `data.status: false` and `otpRequired: false`. Ask the customer to make a new <a href={`${props.base}/save-a-card`}>card-saving payment</a> with that card.

## Charge on a schedule

Nomba doesn't schedule API charges. Your server decides when each customer is due. A common setup is a scheduled job, such as a cron job, that runs every day:

1. Find every customer whose payment is due today.
2. Charge each one with a new `orderReference`.
3. Record the result against the customer's subscription.

### Plan for OTPs during scheduled charges

A scheduled charge runs when the customer isn't using your app. If their bank asks for an OTP, the customer still has to enter it within 15 minutes (see <a href={`${props.base}/complete-otp`}>Complete an OTP challenge</a>), or the charge fails. To give it the best chance of succeeding:

- Run scheduled charges during the day, when customers are likely to see a message.
- When `otpRequired` is `true`, notify the customer straight away, by push notification, SMS or email, with a link to a page where they can enter the code.

## When a charge fails

**Rejected by Nomba (`code` `"400"`).** Nothing was sent to the bank. Common messages:

| `data.message` | Cause |
|---|---|
| `Invalid card token` | The `tokenKey` doesn't exist, was deleted, or was saved by another Nomba account. |
| `Email address doesn't match token key` | `customerEmail` isn't the email the card was saved with. |
| `An order already exists with this order reference` | You've already used this `orderReference`. |
| `Card payment is not supported for this order. Please use a different payment method.` | Card payments aren't enabled for your account. |
| `Customer email is invalid` | `customerEmail` is missing or isn't a valid email address. |

**Declined by the bank (`code` `"00"`, `data.status` `false`).** The bank's reason is in `data.message`, for example `Insufficient funds.`, `Your card has expired. Please use a different card.`, or a general decline. The exact wording depends on the bank and card network.

To retry safely:

- **If you don't know whether a charge went through** (for example, your request timed out), send it again with the **same** `orderReference`. Nomba never charges the same `orderReference` twice. If the first request did reach Nomba, the retry is rejected with "An order already exists with this order reference". <a href={`${props.base}/confirm-the-charge`}>Verify</a> that `orderReference` to see the result.
- **If the bank declined the charge, don't retry straight away.** Wait, for example 24 hours, and stop after a few attempts. Use a new `orderReference` for each new attempt.
- **Ask the customer to update their card** if the charge keeps failing, or if the card has expired. They can add a new card with a new <a href={`${props.base}/save-a-card`}>card-saving payment</a>.
