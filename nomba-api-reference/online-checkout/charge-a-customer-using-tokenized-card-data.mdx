<Note>
Some banks require the customer to enter an OTP before a tokenized card can be charged. When that happens, the response has `otpRequired: true` and a `transactionId`, and the charge stays pending:

1. Collect the OTP the customer receives from their bank.
2. Submit it with [Submit customer card OTP](/nomba-api-reference/charge/submit-customer-card-otp), sending `otp`, `orderReference` and the `transactionId` from this response.

A charge whose OTP is not submitted within about 15 minutes is marked as failed.
</Note>
