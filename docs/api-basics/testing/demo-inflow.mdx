The Nomba Demo Inflow is a sandbox banking app. Use it to play a customer who pays into one of your [simulated virtual accounts](/docs/api-basics/testing/virtual-accounts), the same way they would from their bank app. No real money moves.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a simulated virtual account">
    [Create a virtual account](/docs/api-basics/testing/virtual-accounts#create-a-simulated-virtual-account) on the sandbox base URL and copy its `bankAccountNumber`. Simulated account numbers start with `0`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send a transfer">
    In Demo Inflow below, tap **Transfer**, enter the account number, then enter an amount and any 4-digit PIN. The account name is looked up on Nombank MFB, as it would be in a real bank app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your webhook">
    Your webhook URL, and the account's `callbackUrl` if it has one, get a `payment_success` webhook. The receipt shows the transaction ref to use with [Transaction requery](/nomba-api-reference/requery/transaction-requery).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What Demo Inflow can do

- Pay **SIMULATED** sandbox virtual accounts only. LIVE accounts and production accounts can't be found.
- The sender is the account's own name, the usual case of a customer paying from their own bank. Transfers to accounts with `restrictInflowToAccountName` set to `true` are accepted.
- A transfer to an expired account fails, and no webhook is sent.
- Each transfer can be up to ₦1,000,000.00.

To test a sender name mismatch or other edge cases, call [Simulate an inflow](/nomba-api-reference/virtual-accounts/simulate-inflow), which lets you set the sender's name.
