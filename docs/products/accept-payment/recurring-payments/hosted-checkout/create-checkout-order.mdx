**Step 1 of 5.** Before you can charge a card later, the customer has to pay with it once. Create a checkout order for that first payment, with `tokenizeCard` set to `true`.

<CreateCheckoutOrder base="/docs/products/accept-payment/recurring-payments/hosted-checkout" />

## Send the customer to checkout

Redirect the customer to the `checkoutLink`, or open it in a web view in your app. The customer enters their card details and completes any checks their bank asks for, such as an OTP. When the payment succeeds, Nomba saves the card. You don't need to make any more API calls.
