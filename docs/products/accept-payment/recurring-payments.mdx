Recurring or subscription payments are processed using the `tokenKey` generated when a customer's card was tokenized during checkout.
This allows you to charge the customer securely without asking for card details again.

<Tip>
  Only include `tokenizeCard: true` when creating a checkout order if you intend to use the customer's card for future payments. The returned `tokenKey` from the webhook will then be required for recurring charges.
</Tip>

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Save the customer's card">
    Create a checkout order with `tokenizeCard: true` ([Tokenize Card](/docs/products/accept-payment/create-checkout-order#tokenize-card)). When the customer pays, the webhook returns a `tokenKey`. Save it: you need it to charge the card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Charge the tokenized card">
    Send the `tokenKey` and the order details to [Charge a Tokenized Card](/docs/products/accept-payment/charge-tokenized-card).
    - If `otpRequired` is `false`, the charge is complete. Go to the last step to verify it.
    - If `otpRequired` is `true`, the customer's bank requires an OTP. The charge stays pending and the response includes a `transactionId`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Collect the OTP from the customer">
    The customer's bank sends an OTP to the phone number or email registered with the card. Ask the customer to enter it in your app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit the OTP">
    Send the OTP with the `orderReference` and `transactionId` to [Submit Tokenized Card OTP](/docs/products/accept-payment/submit-tokenized-card-otp).

    A charge whose OTP is not submitted within about 15 minutes is marked as failed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the transaction">
    Confirm the final status before giving value to your customer. You can use webhooks for instant notifications, but it is best practice to confirm the status via the [Verify Transactions](/docs/products/accept-payment/verify-transactions) endpoint.
  </Step>
</Steps>
